Privacy Policy – finarpit uploader

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The finarpit uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @finarpit to upload and manage that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uploads videos, sets their privacy status and adds them to playlists on the owner's channel. It does not access any other user's data.

What it stores

Only the OAuth credentials needed to act for the owner, kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private repository. No personal data of viewers or other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the @finarpit YouTube channel.
